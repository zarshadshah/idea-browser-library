Your Grandma might be the next big brand
6.5
/10

THE IDEA

A hobbyist has a hot sauce or spice blend that friends beg for. They call a co-packer and hit the wall: minimum runs of thousands to tens of thousands of units, three to six month waits at facilities like California Hot Sauce Solutions, and one operator quoting $5,500 just for a pilot run. Marcia's Munchies called nearly every production facility in the country and could not find one whose minimum they could hit, so they had to co-build a factory. Meanwhile 12+ states rewrote cottage food laws in 2024-2025 (Texas SB 541, West Virginia SB 44, North Dakota SB 2386) and TikTok Shop food-category sales more than doubled year over year in 2025. The demand pull is there; the on-ramp is not.

Concierge Launch is a done-for-you service that takes a hobbyist food maker from home recipe to first paid orders in about 90 days. A $497 recipe audit scores commercial viability, flags acidified-food risk under 21 CFR 114, and gives an honest go/no-go. The $9,500 core package scales the recipe, matches a small-batch co-packer with a signed pilot run, ships FDA-compliant labels, files a process authority letter where required, and puts a Shopify storefront live. A $750/month retainer handles reorders, label updates, and one retail-buyer pitch per quarter. The customer is not a nostalgic family; it is the hobbyist CPG founder who already knows they want a brand and is stuck at the factory door.

12+ states rewrote cottage food laws in 2024-2025 while TikTok Shop food sales doubled

Two waves are hitting at the same time. First, states are rewriting their cottage food laws faster than at any point in history. At least a dozen states passed significant expansions in 2024-2025. Texas SB 541 (September 2025) lets a registered cottage food vendor sell in any retail store. West Virginia SB 44 (June 2026) legalized selling acidified, pickled, and fermented foods from the home, the exact category that used to force a founder into a co-packer and an FDA process-authority letter. North Dakota SB 2386 (March 2025) opened online, phone, mail, and consignment sales. Washington DC amended its law in June 2025 to allow wholesale to retail. The on-ramp from home kitchen to first paying customer is shorter and cheaper than it has ever been.

Second, the exit ramp to real volume just appeared. TikTok Shop US food-category sales more than doubled year over year in 2025. TikTok cites 103 billion US searches with purchase intent, transaction volume up nearly 80%, and enterprise-brand sales up 97%. The digital-to-retail path for a new food brand has compressed to 18 months: creator-led launch generates the velocity signal, Whole Foods and Target buyers track it, shelf space follows. The specialty food industry is now $219 billion and still forecast to grow 5.5% in 2025. Small-batch co-packers built for this wave are already at capacity: California Hot Sauce Solutions posts a 3-6 month wait for new clients, and new small-batch entrants like Kensington Food are opening facilities specifically for founders with low minimums.

WHY NOW

12+ states rewrote cottage food laws in 2024-2025 while TikTok Shop food sales doubled

Two waves are hitting at the same time. First, states are rewriting their cottage food laws faster than at any point in history. At least a dozen states passed significant expansions in 2024-2025. Texas SB 541 (September 2025) lets a registered cottage food vendor sell in any retail store. West Virginia SB 44 (June 2026) legalized selling acidified, pickled, and fermented foods from the home, the exact category that used to force a founder into a co-packer and an FDA process-authority letter. North Dakota SB 2386 (March 2025) opened online, phone, mail, and consignment sales. Washington DC amended its law in June 2025 to allow wholesale to retail. The on-ramp from home kitchen to first paying customer is shorter and cheaper than it has ever been.

Second, the exit ramp to real volume just appeared. TikTok Shop US food-category sales more than doubled year over year in 2025. TikTok cites 103 billion US searches with purchase intent, transaction volume up nearly 80%, and enterprise-brand sales up 97%. The digital-to-retail path for a new food brand has compressed to 18 months: creator-led launch generates the velocity signal, Whole Foods and Target buyers track it, shelf space follows. The specialty food industry is now $219 billion and still forecast to grow 5.5% in 2025. Small-batch co-packers built for this wave are already at capacity: California Hot Sauce Solutions posts a 3-6 month wait for new clients, and new small-batch entrants like Kensington Food are opening facilities specifically for founders with low minimums.
