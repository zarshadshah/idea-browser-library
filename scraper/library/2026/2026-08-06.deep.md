Verified clinic comparison for hair transplant buyers
⏰
Perfect Timing
⚡
Unfair Advantage
+17 More

Men researching a hair transplant scroll through clinic ads dressed up as honest reviews. One clinic advertises $2,000 in Turkey, another $15,000 in the States, and the details that separate them stay buried. Who holds the scalpel, what the revision policy covers, what the result looks like a year out. Guys stake a five-figure decision on a marketing photo.

Folliqo verifies each clinic against primary sources, from licensing boards to real patient timelines to the contract's revision terms. Each profile carries four required disclosures, the all-in price per graft, the named surgeon, the written revision policy, and patient photos at a year instead of same-day shots. TransplantMatch matches buyers to clinics but runs on the clinics' own marketing, so the polished photos survive the trip. Folliqo marks what a clinic leaves blank, so buyers see what it would rather keep quiet.

Verify twenty to thirty high-volume clinics across Turkey and the States to start. Each one gets a row in an Airtable database with the disclosure fields locked in. Content breaking down what clinics hide, formatted for search and Reddit, pulls the first buyers in. Hardest part is the clinic-side cold start, solved by trading early visibility to three clinics willing to go on record. Answer questions in r/HairTransplants and land ten subscribers by being the first source treating this as a medical decision.

Buyers pay the least of anyone on this platform. Basic runs $10 a month for verified clinic data and ratings, and $30 adds long-term outcome tracking and analytics. Clinics under the microscope pay the freight, $499 a month for a compliance badge and preferred placement once they pass. Growth comes from owning the long-tail searches around real results and surgeon credentials that a nervous buyer types at midnight. Each new country deepens the dataset that makes the platform worth trusting.
