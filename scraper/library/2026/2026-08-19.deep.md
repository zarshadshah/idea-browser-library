Checkout testing for stores that sell to AI agents
⏰
Perfect Timing
⚡
Unfair Advantage
+14 More

Shopify storefronts are built for human eyes. Images persuade, layouts guide, banners manufacture urgency. Shopping agents skip the surface and read the data underneath. Prices read ambiguous, coupon logic assumes a person, and one inventory field is formatted three ways across the catalog. Checkout stalls. The conversion report shows the drop and stops there.

Cartness runs a Shopify store through the behavior of a shopping agent: browsing, adding to cart, applying promos, calculating shipping, pushing to checkout. Friction points get flagged, from coupon rules that break under machine logic to checkout fields that stall the purchase. The store gets a readiness score and a prioritized fix list. Katalon and the QA field automate tests written for human journeys, leaving the machine buyer's read of the catalog untested. Monitoring continues as the catalog shifts, since each new product description can break the read.

Five core scenarios carry the suite: a simple purchase, a coupon, a multi-item cart, a subscription signup, and an international order. Playwright or Puppeteer drive each flow, with the scripts reading product pages the way a language model would, logging where the data goes two ways. The judgment call is which breaks cost a sale. Three live stores settle that fast, because the same failures repeat across catalogs. Fix recommendations come from observed breaks, so the report hands developers real work.

One recovered checkout path pays for the $200 monthly trial, which is why Shopify Plus merchants buy first. The $500 tier adds full suites and continuous monitoring for catalogs that move weekly. Shopify agencies make the strongest channel, since the client relationship is theirs and they need something to sell. Teardown videos handle the rest: an agent failing to buy from a known store is the pitch. Conversion teams are adopting the check as a pre-launch step, and stores that pass collect the traffic others drop.

*Analysis, scores, and revenue estimates are educational and based on assumptions. Results vary by execution and market conditions.
