Daily health decision text message app
7.0
/10

THE IDEA

A Whoop member posted three days ago that he will not renew year two because the built-in AI coach is useless at £27 a month. An Oura owner in the same week said screenshotting data into ChatGPT gives a better read than the paid Advisor. r/ouraring has 231K members and r/whoop has 138K, and both run fresh complaint threads about the coach that came with the ring or the strap. 46% of US adults now own a wearable and 83% wear it five days a week. The buyer trusts the data. The buyer does not trust the advice on top of it.

Recovery Desk is a standing agent that reads your Oura or Whoop overnight and sends one decision text every morning before you open any app: train hard, back off, eat more, sleep earlier, plus a one-line why. No dashboard. No chat window. One persona at launch (endurance athletes), $29 a month, cancel by texting STOP. Bevel already earns an estimated $1.13M a month on the App Store selling an AI coach on this exact data, so the willingness to pay is proven. The wedge is the delivery: a text, not another chart.
