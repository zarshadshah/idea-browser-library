Tool to connect video views to home sales
6.8
/10

THE IDEA

63% of US real estate agents publish video to sell listings. NAR's 2025 Technology Survey says social is now their number one lead source at 39%, ahead of the CRM (23%) and the MLS (17%). 52% say social leads close better than the ones they buy from portals. But the agent posts a listing reel, gets 47 views and zero DMs, and cannot tell whether the hook failed, the price band failed, or Instagram just did not push it. The lead cycle runs 4 to 12 weeks, so by the time a deal closes nobody remembers which reel started it.

This idea is an analytics layer that connects the agent's social accounts, the MLS listing record, and the brokerage CRM, then tells the brokerage which videos actually created inquiries, showings, and closed deals. Sell a $1,500 audit that pulls 6 months of posts against real leads, then convert to $19 per agent per month for the ongoing dashboard. Higher tiers add Follow Up Boss and kvCORE writes, MLS listing matches, and a local benchmark showing how your hooks rank in the metro.
