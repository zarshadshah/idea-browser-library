Tune-up shop for misfiring business chatbots
⏰
Perfect Timing
⚡
Unfair Advantage
+13 More

Chatbots went live in 2023, and the team moved on. Two years later the bot still answers on demand, fast and confident, and quotes prices that expired last spring. Logs go unread for months. Each wrong answer at 11pm sends a real buyer to the competitor who picked up the phone. Set and forget turned into set and bleed.

Rebot is the tune-up shop for misfiring chatbots. It stress-tests a deployed bot with the inputs real customers send: dialects, slang, anger, jailbreak attempts. Stale training data, cluttered context, and broken prompt logic show up in the logs and get fixed in place, retuned skills pushed live with a report of what changed. Gorgias sells businesses the bot and the tools to build it, and repairing one drifting in production is the job it leaves open. By the next morning the same bot answers right again.

Start by hand: a Typeform intake and a human reading the logs of one live bot. Build a stress-test suite that fires real failure modes, edge cases, lost context, policy drift, and log what breaks. Ship ten tune-ups and record each failure pattern, because the patterns are the product. Hardest part is reproducing a misfire on demand, since a bot that fails at 11pm behaves in a clean test. Pattern libraries compound with each repair, so the fiftieth tune-up runs faster than the first.

Repairs start as one-off jobs at $100 a session, and the fixes worth keeping convert to a $299 monthly retainer for monitoring and quarterly retunes. Agency white-label deals let shops resell tune-ups under their own brand, a channel running on someone else's sales team. r/sysadmin, IT Slack groups, and SMB forums surface chatbot complaint threads weekly, pointing the funnel at owners feeling the pain. Each completed tune feeds a performance dataset that becomes the benchmark bot platforms pay to license.
