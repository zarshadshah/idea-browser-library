WHY NOW

AICPA stood up an AI assurance standard in October 2026; firms must prove AI, not claim it

Three timing shifts land inside one 12-month window. First, the profession's own body moved. On October 19, 2026 the AICPA and AIUC launched a joint SOC 2 plus AIUC-1 assurance certification: a CPA-led engagement that live-tests AI agents across thousands of scenarios, with quarterly technical retesting and annual renewal. Multiple CPA firms are already delivering it. The full launch is set for early 2027. The idea that an accounting firm's AI gets a standardized practical test is now a sanctioned concept, not a startup pitch.

Second, buyer pressure is documented at the top of the market. Thomson Reuters' Future of Professionals Report 2026 (June 22, 2026, 1,816 professionals across 62 countries) found 78% of corporate clients call AI-enabled quality essential or very important, 6% say most providers deliver it, and 32% will reconsider provider relationships within 12 months. Thomson Reuters put ~$143B in US legal and accounting revenue under active reconsideration based on AI delivery. The gap is real at the enterprise level; small-business owner pressure is much softer, which is why this idea's wedge is upmarket, not Main Street.

Three timing shifts land inside one 12-month window. First, the profession's own body moved. On October 19, 2026 the AICPA and AIUC launched a joint SOC 2 plus AIUC-1 assurance certification: a CPA-led engagement that live-tests AI agents across thousands of scenarios, with quarterly technical retesting and annual renewal. Multiple CPA firms are already delivering it. The full launch is set for early 2027. The idea that an accounting firm's AI gets a standardized practical test is now a sanctioned concept, not a startup pitch.

Second, buyer pressure is documented at the top of the market. Thomson Reuters' Future of Professionals Report 2026 (June 22, 2026, 1,816 professionals across 62 countries) found 78% of corporate clients call AI-enabled quality essential or very important, 6% say most providers deliver it, and 32% will reconsider provider relationships within 12 months. Thomson Reuters put ~$143B in US legal and accounting revenue under active reconsideration based on AI delivery. The gap is real at the enterprise level; small-business owner pressure is much softer, which is why this idea's wedge is upmarket, not Main Street.
