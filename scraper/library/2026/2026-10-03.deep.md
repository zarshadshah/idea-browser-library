What's your boring business worth?
6.5
/10

THE IDEA

A roofing owner doing $8M in revenue has one shot at a liquidity event, and the gap between a 3x and a 12x EBITDA multiple on the same business is life-changing money. In 2026, 56 named private-equity roll-up platforms are hunting roofing companies, three deals closed in a single week of September, and buyers now publish exact criteria for the premium multiple: recurring re-roofs, commercial mix, insurance-program revenue, and an operator who can leave. Most owners cannot see which of these they are missing until an advisor runs a report once, 90 days before the sale, and by then the fix takes two years.

The product is a vertical exit dashboard for roofing, restoration, and remediation owners doing $2M-$25M. It plugs into QuickBooks, JobNimbus or AccuLynx, and claim data to produce a live monthly valuation, a buyer-readiness score against 18 tracked PE platforms, and a prioritized action plan to raise the multiple. A free valuation report pulls the owner in. A $499/mo dashboard keeps them honest between now and sale. A $15K pre-sale sprint runs the actual exit when the window opens.

PE roll-up platforms in roofing grew 229% in two years, and buyers now pay 3x-12x for the same EBITDA

The buyers showed up and stayed. Named roofing roll-up platforms grew from 17 at the start of 2023 to 56 by end of 2024, and PE-backed firms now hold 7 of the top 10 US roofing contractors. Three deals closed in a single week this September: Tecta America (Altas Partners) bought Babilla Roofing on September 28, Ridgeline Roofing (Bertram Capital) closed its 8th add-on on September 24, and Royalty Roofing announced its 4th acquisition of the year. The pace is not slowing: over $50B of PE capital has flowed into home-services roll-ups since 2018, and five platforms recapitalized at $20B+ in combined value between November 2024 and February 2026, including Apollo's $10B round into Apex Service Partners and Blackstone's $2.5B buy of Champions Group at 18.5x EBITDA.

Here is why that creates urgency for the owner. Roofing in 2026 trades across a 3x-12x EBITDA spread on identical earnings. A generic owner-operator roofer prices at 3-5x; a multi-state regional with recurring maintenance mix clears 6-8x; a premium platform with program revenue hits 8-12x. On a $1M EBITDA restoration shop, the mix difference alone (commercial and insurance-program vs residential storm-chase) is a $2M swing in sale price. Multiples have compressed about one turn from the 2022 peak and roofing sits at the low end of the home-services pack (3-5x vs HVAC 5-10x), so the window to earn the premium instead of take the discount is open now and tightening. At the same time, nearly half of US small-business owners are 55+ and only 54% have a succession plan; McKinsey projects annual small-business exits could run 42% above 2011 levels by 2035.

WHY NOW

PE roll-up platforms in roofing grew 229% in two years, and buyers now pay 3x-12x for the same EBITDA

The buyers showed up and stayed. Named roofing roll-up platforms grew from 17 at the start of 2023 to 56 by end of 2024, and PE-backed firms now hold 7 of the top 10 US roofing contractors. Three deals closed in a single week this September: Tecta America (Altas Partners) bought Babilla Roofing on September 28, Ridgeline Roofing (Bertram Capital) closed its 8th add-on on September 24, and Royalty Roofing announced its 4th acquisition of the year. The pace is not slowing: over $50B of PE capital has flowed into home-services roll-ups since 2018, and five platforms recapitalized at $20B+ in combined value between November 2024 and February 2026, including Apollo's $10B round into Apex Service Partners and Blackstone's $2.5B buy of Champions Group at 18.5x EBITDA.

Here is why that creates urgency for the owner. Roofing in 2026 trades across a 3x-12x EBITDA spread on identical earnings. A generic owner-operator roofer prices at 3-5x; a multi-state regional with recurring maintenance mix clears 6-8x; a premium platform with program revenue hits 8-12x. On a $1M EBITDA restoration shop, the mix difference alone (commercial and insurance-program vs residential storm-chase) is a $2M swing in sale price. Multiples have compressed about one turn from the 2022 peak and roofing sits at the low end of the home-services pack (3-5x vs HVAC 5-10x), so the window to earn the premium instead of take the discount is open now and tightening. At the same time, nearly half of US small-business owners are 55+ and only 54% have a succession plan; McKinsey projects annual small-business exits could run 42% above 2011 levels by 2035.
