Contractors can print you $$$
5.8
/10

THE IDEA

AI labs ran out of clean web text this year. Stanford's 2026 AI Index calls it 'peak data.' At the same time, US AI copyright suits tripled from 45 to 140 in 14 months, Anthropic paid $1.5B in the Bartz settlement, and physical AI raised $6.4B in Q1 2026 alone. Buyers now want rights-cleared records of how physical work actually happens. Meanwhile a 30-truck roofing owner is sitting on 12 years of job photos, estimates, and inspection notes he cannot even export from ServiceTitan without hiring a lawyer.

The idea is a commission agency that represents that contractor. The founder audits the archive, de-identifies the PII, packages a buyer-ready sample, and places it through existing marketplaces (Troveo, Datarade) or direct AI-buyer BD. Contractor keeps 70-80% of any deal, the founder takes 20-30%, and the contractor never has to become a data company. A paid archive audit at $2K-$5K funds the work whether a license ever closes or not.
