Run the refund desk contractors don't have
6.8
/10

THE IDEA

A roofing or HVAC pro spends $2,000 to $10,000 a month buying leads on Angi, Thumbtack, HomeAdvisor, Bark and Google Local Services Ads. One in ten to one in five leads is fake, out of area, or the wrong service. The pro fights for refunds one at a time. A Thumbtack pro tallied $27,000 in denied refunds over 2.5 years. Another burned $1,200 in three months on Angi where two of every three leads were fraudulent. HomeAdvisor logged more than 1,200 pro complaints at the BBB in three years and only refunds in expiring platform credit.

The product classifies every purchased lead using call transcripts and lead records, then files evidence-backed refund requests inside each platform's window (Angi 30 days, Thumbtack 45), and reconciles Google LSA charges against the ML auto-credits that show up weeks later. Pricing is $149 to $399 a month by lead volume, sold behind a free 90-day audit that returns one dollar number in 48 hours. Recover 15% of a $5,000 monthly lead spend and the top tier pays back four times over.
