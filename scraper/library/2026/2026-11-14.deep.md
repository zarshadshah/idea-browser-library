Both land on a bettor who has never had to math this out before. Under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act section 70114, only 90% of wagering losses are deductible in 2026, still capped at winnings. A bettor who wagered $100K, won $100K, and lost $100K owes tax on $10K they never kept. This is the first year the rule applies. Every CPA guide calls it phantom income.

Congress is trying to kill it, and failing. On September 16, 2026 House Ways and Means voted 38-5 to advance a repeal as Title VII of H.R. 10357, the Digital Asset Tax Certainty Act. The bill has still not passed the House floor or the Senate. On November 5, 2026 Rep. Dina Titus sent a public letter to House leadership asking them to attach the fix to a must-pass year-end vehicle. As of today, the 90% rule is the law a 2026 filer must plan around. Even if repeal passes in December, every bettor needs the number computed both ways to size the Q4 payment. Underneath all of this, prediction markets are a new $187B wallet with zero IRS guidance: Kalshi issues no 1099-B on trading profit and loss, Polymarket and Robinhood event contracts send nothing, and the IRS has not ruled whether these are gambling, capital gains, or Section 1256. WHY NOW

90% gambling loss cap is live law for 2026 with 10 weeks until Q4 estimates are due

Two clocks are running at once. The first is the Q4 2026 estimated tax payment due January 15, 2027. The second is the April 15, 2027 filing deadline. Both land on a bettor who has never had to math this out before. Under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act section 70114, only 90% of wagering losses are deductible in 2026, still capped at winnings. A bettor who wagered $100K, won $100K, and lost $100K owes tax on $10K they never kept. This is the first year the rule applies. Every CPA guide calls it phantom income.

Congress is trying to kill it, and failing. On September 16, 2026 House Ways and Means voted 38-5 to advance a repeal as Title VII of H.R. 10357, the Digital Asset Tax Certainty Act. The bill has still not passed the House floor or the Senate. On November 5, 2026 Rep. Dina Titus sent a public letter to House leadership asking them to attach the fix to a must-pass year-end vehicle. As of today, the 90% rule is the law a 2026 filer must plan around. Even if repeal passes in December, every bettor needs the number computed both ways to size the Q4 payment. Underneath all of this, prediction markets are a new $187B wallet with zero IRS guidance: Kalshi issues no 1099-B on trading profit and loss, Polymarket and Robinhood event contracts send nothing, and the IRS has not ruled whether these are gambling, capital gains, or Section 1256. First filing season for this entire category hits spring 2027.
