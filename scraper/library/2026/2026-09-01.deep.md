6.5
/10

THE IDEA

A prospect used to ask a friend for a designer. Now they ask ChatGPT. BrightLocal's 2026 survey says 45% of consumers used AI to find a business recommendation in the past year, up from 6% the year before. ChatGPT hit 900 million weekly active users in February 2026, and its outbound referral traffic grew 206% year over year. The solo freelancer who used to win on referrals now competes inside a five-name list generated by a model that has never met them. Eight tools track who wins that list. None get a named freelancer into it.

This is a done-for-you AI search publicist for one person. The buyer enters their name and category into a free audit and sees the current ChatGPT answer, the third-party pages feeding it, and the sentence their own site is missing. A $299 Placement Sprint runs 21 days and gets them into three source pages ChatGPT already cites: a directory, a roundup, a curated list. The $149 monthly retainer re-checks answers across ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, ships one new placement per month, and maintains a structured profile page. Priced $50 above the monitoring tools that only ship a score, priced 10x below the GEO agencies that start at $1,500 a month and only sell to brands.
