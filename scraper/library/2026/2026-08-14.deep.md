Local mom concierge for out-of-town college parents
⏰
Perfect Timing
👑
Proven Founder Fit
+16 More

College parents live hundreds of miles from campus. Worry runs in the background all semester. Car batteries die at 11pm with nobody nearby to help. Kids catch the flu and skip the health center. Birthdays happen over a video call. Facebook groups are full of strangers and TaskRabbit feels cold. Parents want one local person who knows the campus and treats their kid like family. Someone like that is hard to find.

Proxiparent gives each family one vetted local mom on their kid's campus. She jump-starts a dead car battery and drops off soup during flu season. She delivers birthday cakes and picks up prescriptions. Dorm moves and finals-week care packages get handled too. MindyKnows pushes info and alerts to a parent's phone but sends nobody in person. Parents pay a yearly membership plus an hourly fee for each task. So the kid has a trusted adult ten minutes from the dorm.

Recruit three to five local moms in different college towns. Give each one a Notion playbook and a Stripe checkout link. Point them at the parent Facebook groups where anxious families ask strangers for help. Let them sell memberships by hand while the software gets built. Hardest part is trust, since parents are handing a stranger access to their kid. Software comes next for the dashboard and the billing and the task requests. Growth comes from word of mouth in parent groups and operator referrals.

Membership runs $325 a semester or $525 a year. Task fees add $60 an hour on top of the base. Retention is emotional because the anxiety comes back each semester. Margins hold because one operator runs a whole campus. Scale one campus at a time and let operator referrals open the next town. Parent orientation weekends are a second way in. Each mom who does well recruits the mom for the next school.

*Analysis, scores, and revenue estimates are educational and based on assumptions. Results vary by execution and market conditions.
