Run for Good - Marathon Charity Platform
6.8
/10

THE IDEA

New York Road Runners took 1% of 240,000 applicants for the 2026 TCS NYC Marathon. London drew 1.33 million ballot entries for 2027, up 18% in a year. Chicago jumped 25% to over 200,000 applications in one cycle. The back door is now the only door, and runners know it: on X right now, people are publicly begging strangers for $4,000 in seven days to hit a Team for Kids minimum. On r/UKRunners the morning after the ballot, small charity trustees post asking any rejected runner to take their place.

The product is a two-sided matching portal for the small-charity long tail across the six Abbott Majors. Charities list open bibs with minimum, deposit, deadline, and spots left. Runners get a free directory plus $29/year alerts the moment a bib opens under their race, minimum, and deadline. Charities pay $99/mo to run the portal plus $150 per filled bib, which beats the $600-$1,040 per-bib fee NYRR already charges them to be in the Official Charity Partner Program.

Charity bibs are the pressure valve, and the money proves it: Chicago's 2025 charity program raised a record $47.1 million, up $11 million in a single year. 6.8

THE IDEA

New York Road Runners took 1% of 240,000 applicants for the 2026 TCS NYC Marathon. London drew 1.33 million ballot entries for 2027, up 18% in a year. Chicago jumped 25% to over 200,000 applications in one cycle. The back door is now the only door, and runners know it: on X right now, people are publicly begging strangers for $4,000 in seven days to hit a Team for Kids minimum. On r/UKRunners the morning after the ballot, small charity trustees post asking any rejected runner to take their place.

The product is a two-sided matching portal for the small-charity long tail across the six Abbott Majors. Charities list open bibs with minimum, deposit, deadline, and spots left. Runners get a free directory plus $29/year alerts the moment a bib opens under their race, minimum, and deadline. Charities pay $99/mo to run the portal plus $150 per filled bib, which beats the $600-$1,040 per-bib fee NYRR already charges them to be in the Official Charity Partner Program.

WHY NOW

NYC took 1% of 240,000

The back door is now the only door for most runners. New York Road Runners accepted about 1% of 240,000 applicants for the 2026 TCS NYC Marathon. London drew 1.33 million ballot entries for 2027, up 18% in a year and more than double the 578,374 from 2024. Chicago jumped from 160,000 applications to over 200,000 for 2026, a 25% rise in one cycle. Sydney, the newest Major, drew 123,000 in its first Abbott-badge ballot. The pattern is not one race. It is every race, every year, getting worse.

Two forces drive it, and both keep running. First, the running boom is still accelerating. Running USA counts growth across every race distance from 2025 to 2024, US marathon finishers hit 432,562 in 2024 (past pre-Covid highs), and more than 40% of London's 1.33 million applicants were first-timers. New runners, not jaded repeats, are the fuel. Second, race fields cannot grow with demand. London added a few thousand places and still had to reject 99.5% of ballots. Charity bibs are the pressure valve, and the money proves it: Chicago's 2025 charity program raised a record $47.1 million, up $11 million in a single year. The infrastructure to match these runners to small charities has not scaled with the pain.
