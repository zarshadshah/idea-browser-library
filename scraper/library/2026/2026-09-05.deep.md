Help therapists accept insurance quicker
6.8
/10

THE IDEA

A newly licensed therapist finishes supervision, hangs a shingle, and learns she cannot bill Aetna or Blue Cross for 90 to 180 days. Only 55 to 60% of behavioral health providers are in-network today, versus about 90% of primary care physicians, and credentialing friction is the reason. A four-month lapse costs a solo therapist roughly $60,000 in unrecoverable revenue. Credentialing backlogs grew nearly 22% in the last year, hitting behavioral health hardest. The word therapists use is nightmare.

This product is a browser agent that files and maintains insurance credentialing under the therapist's own NPI. Panel packs cost $199 for three payers, $349 for five, $499 multi-state, matching what GetPaneled and other per-panel shops already charge. The real SKU is recurring: $99 per month for a solo practice, or $49 per provider per month for group practices of 2 to 15 clinicians, covering CAQH re-attestation, expirables, re-credentialing, and roster audits. A human reviews every submission before it goes out.

THE IDEA

A newly licensed therapist finishes supervision, hangs a shingle, and learns she cannot bill Aetna or Blue Cross for 90 to 180 days. Only 55 to 60% of behavioral health providers are in-network today, versus about 90% of primary care physicians, and credentialing friction is the reason. A four-month lapse costs a solo therapist roughly $60,000 in unrecoverable revenue. Credentialing backlogs grew nearly 22% in the last year, hitting behavioral health hardest. The word therapists use is nightmare.

This product is a browser agent that files and maintains insurance credentialing under the therapist's own NPI. Panel packs cost $199 for three payers, $349 for five, $499 multi-state, matching what GetPaneled and other per-panel shops already charge. The real SKU is recurring: $99 per month for a solo practice, or $49 per provider per month for group practices of 2 to 15 clinicians, covering CAQH re-attestation, expirables, re-credentialing, and roster audits. A human reviews every submission before it goes out.

WHY NOW

Backlogs up 22%, token prices down 99.7%, form-filling agents finally shipped

Three lines crossed in 2026 that did not cross before. The pain got worse: a healthcare operations survey found provider credentialing backlogs grew nearly 22% in the past year, and the report calls out behavioral health and telehealth as the hardest hit. Only 55 to 60% of behavioral health providers are in-network today, versus roughly 90% of primary care physicians, and the gap is credentialing friction, not clinician supply. HRSA projects demand for behavioral health services will rise 49% through 2033 while workforce supply grows only 11%, a 4.5-to-1 ratio. Every year that gap widens, more new therapists open their own doors and hit this wall on day one.

The tools to fix it shipped this year. Anthropic launched Claude in Chrome in 2026, a browser extension that fills forms and runs multi-step workflows. Skyvern 2.0 scored 85.85% on the WebVoyager benchmark and is called out by name for insurance forms and government portals, the exact shape of payer applications. Token prices collapsed alongside: GPT-4 launched at $30 per million input tokens in March 2023; Gemini 3.1 Flash cost $0.10 per million by April 2026, a 99.7% drop. OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Luna another 80% in July 2026. A 90-day, 12-payer credentialing job that would have cost thousands of dollars in tokens two years ago now costs single-digit dollars, so a $199 per-panel price point actually has margin.
