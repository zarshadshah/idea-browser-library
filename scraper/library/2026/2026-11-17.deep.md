WHY NOW

Four things just lined up at once. First, the price of the replacement collapsed. An October 17, 2026 check of six AI voice platforms put a production voice agent at $0.08 to $0.14 per minute all-in, phone line included. A human call-center minute still costs $0.50 to $1.50 fully loaded. That is a 3-10x gap on the single biggest front-office line a solo dental or law office pays for.

Second, the incumbents keep raising. Per-seat legal SaaS from Clio, MyCase, NetDocuments and Relativity went up 15% to 30% a year from 2023 through 2026, three straight renewal rounds, against 4-7% firm revenue growth. Across SaaS more broadly, list-price hikes landed at 7-12% in 2026 while actual paid prices climbed up to 30% at renewal. Solo owners feel this as the Clio bill jumping to $1,609 a year per user and the annual renewal letter they already dread.
