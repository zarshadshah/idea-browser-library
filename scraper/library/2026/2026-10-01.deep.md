A service for freelancers who ghosted the IRS
7.3
/10

THE IDEA

A 43-year-old freelancer making $62,000 a year admits on record he has not filed for five years and loses sleep over jail. A working professional on Blind opens with shame and asks if H&R Block or a CPA will judge him for six years unfiled. A Ramsey caller three years sober is finally ready to fix the years she and her husband skipped during his alcoholism. The IRS mailed 3,157,055 CP59 non-filer notices in fiscal year 2025, up from zero two years earlier, and its staff is down 27%. The people getting those letters are searching Reddit and YouTube at 2am, then bouncing off Optima's $4,000 average price with a $10,000 debt minimum.

This is a flat-fee back-tax cleanup for self-employed people three to ten years behind. One published price, $599 per return year, no minimum debt, no sales call to see the number. An enrolled agent pulls the free IRS wage-and-income transcript, uses AI to reconcile it against a short expense workbook, signs the returns, and files a payment plan or first-time penalty abatement. Six years for $3,594, roughly one fifth of what a tax relief firm quotes for the same case.

IRS mailed 3.15M non-filer notices in FY25, up from zero two years earlier

For two straight fiscal years the IRS mailed no CP59 non-filer notices at all. Then in February 2024 it restarted the program. The Treasury Inspector General reported 1,679,141 CP59 notices in fiscal year 2024 and 3,157,055 in fiscal year 2025. That is zero to 3.15 million in 24 months. The current wave uses AI-assisted matching against 1099s and W-2s, which is exactly the paper trail a freelancer leaves when clients report payments but the freelancer never filed.

At the same time the IRS is smaller and slower. The agency lost 28,312 employees between January 2025 and March 2026, a 27% cut, with revenue agents down about 31%. IRS Direct File, the free government filing tool, was officially cancelled for the 2026 season in a November 3, 2025 email to the 25 partner states, with no relaunch date set. And starting January 1, 2026, the OBBBA raised the 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC threshold from $600 to $2,000, so freelancers will get fewer paper forms in the mail even though the tax is still owed. Fewer forms plus fewer humans at the IRS plus more AI-driven letters means more freelancers get caught with weaker paper trails and less free help. That is the buyer this service exists for.

WHY NOW

IRS mailed 3.15M non-filer notices in FY25, up from zero two years earlier

For two straight fiscal years the IRS mailed no CP59 non-filer notices at all. Then in February 2024 it restarted the program. The Treasury Inspector General reported 1,679,141 CP59 notices in fiscal year 2024 and 3,157,055 in fiscal year 2025. That is zero to 3.15 million in 24 months. The current wave uses AI-assisted matching against 1099s and W-2s, which is exactly the paper trail a freelancer leaves when clients report payments but the freelancer never filed.

At the same time the IRS is smaller and slower. The agency lost 28,312 employees between January 2025 and March 2026, a 27% cut, with revenue agents down about 31%. IRS Direct File, the free government filing tool, was officially cancelled for the 2026 season in a November 3, 2025 email to the 25 partner states, with no relaunch date set. And starting January 1, 2026, the OBBBA raised the 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC threshold from $600 to $2,000, so freelancers will get fewer paper forms in the mail even though the tax is still owed. Fewer forms plus fewer humans at the IRS plus more AI-driven letters means more freelancers get caught with weaker paper trails and less free help. That is the buyer this service exists for.
