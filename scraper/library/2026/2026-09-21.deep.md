The Pop-up Conversion shop (lead capture games for small brands)
6.8
/10

THE IDEA

Meta cost per action rose 38.1% in one year to $38.19, and Apple Mail Privacy plus iOS 26 Digest View broke email metrics along the way. DTC brands doing under $10M pay $15 to $299 a month for the same spin wheel popup every other store runs (Wheelio alone sits on about 2,613 Shopify stores). It works (merchants report 5% to 14% signup rates), but it looks like everyone else and it caps at the template.

This is a done-for-you branded browser game shipped as a lead magnet. A small studio-in-a-box reskins a proven mini-game mechanic in the brand's art, ties it to a scaling discount, hosts it, and exports every email and phone number to Klaviyo, Postscript, or Attentive. $1,500 to $5,000 per campaign setup, $99 to $299 a month to keep it live. Sold first to the small DTC agencies that already run the brand's paid social and want a service they can white-label.

The Pop-up Conversion shop (lead capture games for small brands)
6.8
/10

THE IDEA

Meta cost per action rose 38.1% in one year to $38.19, and Apple Mail Privacy plus iOS 26 Digest View broke email metrics along the way. DTC brands doing under $10M pay $15 to $299 a month for the same spin wheel popup every other store runs (Wheelio alone sits on about 2,613 Shopify stores). It works (merchants report 5% to 14% signup rates), but it looks like everyone else and it caps at the template.

This is a done-for-you branded browser game shipped as a lead magnet. A small studio-in-a-box reskins a proven mini-game mechanic in the brand's art, ties it to a scaling discount, hosts it, and exports every email and phone number to Klaviyo, Postscript, or Attentive. $1,500 to $5,000 per campaign setup, $99 to $299 a month to keep it live. Sold first to the small DTC agencies that already run the brand's paid social and want a service they can white-label.

WHY NOW

Meta CPA up 38% in one year while AI makes custom browser games cheap

The math on paid acquisition got worse fast. Meta average CPM rose 20.1% from 2025 to 2026 and cost per action rose 38.1% from $27.66 to $38.19. Q4 2025 ecommerce CPMs hit all-time highs near $23. DTC brands doing $2M-$20M in revenue cannot outbid Temu and Shein in the auction, so they need cheaper ways to fill the top of the funnel. An email or SMS captured on-site at 10% conversion costs a fraction of a paid lead, and merchants on Wheelio report exactly that lift.

The supply side changed too. Y Combinator said 25% of its Winter 2025 batch shipped codebases that were 95% AI-generated, and Collins named vibe coding its 2025 word of the year. WebGPU shipped across major browsers in September 2025. A custom-branded browser game that used to need a MarketJS license at $2K-$16K plus a developer now takes a solo builder with Claude and Cursor a weekend to reskin. That is the price collapse that makes a $1,500-$5,000 done-for-you campaign work as a business, not a favor.
