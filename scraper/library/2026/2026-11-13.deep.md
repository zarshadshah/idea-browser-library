WHY NOW

Scans up 20% YoY, 63% of owners say staffing is their top risk

The digital evidence a desk needs to review a shift now exists at scale. From October 2024 through March 2026, Janitorial Manager customers alone ran more than 2.2 million QR-code service scans, and monthly scan volume grew nearly 20% year-over-year. The US janitorial software market reached $0.85B in 2025 and is on track to $2.1B by 2034. Photo proof, GPS check-ins, and zone-level completion records are now shipped by the apps owners already pay for.

At the same time, buyers moved the goal posts. Proof of service shows up as a baseline qualification in healthcare, education, and food service RFPs, and outcome-based SLAs ("all zones serviced to defined standards, with documented proof, reviewed monthly") are replacing hours-and-headcount contracts. The reporting layer is now part of what the client is buying, not a bonus.
