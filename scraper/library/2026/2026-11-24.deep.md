WHY NOW

Capture costs dropped to pennies per part while 25.75% of Americans got right-to-repair protection

The capture stack a solo builder needs for this product stopped being expensive this year. On-device OCR reads 94.1% of fields correctly on clean phone-camera photos, instant, free, and offline. Whisper voice transcription is $0.006 per minute, with gpt-4o-mini-transcribe at $0.003 ($0.18 per hour). GPT-4o-mini vision costs roughly $0.0001 to $0.0003 per 1024 pixel image, and Gemini 2.0 Flash is cheaper still. A shop that photographs a data plate and a part, and dictates 30 seconds of condition notes on 100 parts per day, runs under 20 cents per day in inference. Two years ago that same workflow needed custom models, server costs, and a six-figure build.

At the same time, used appliance parts just became a bigger, more protected business. 13 states have enacted right-to-repair laws as of July 2026, up from 4 in 2023. About 25.75% of Americans now live under an enforceable law. California's Right to Repair Act explicitly covers appliances with a 7-year parts obligation. Colorado banned parts pairing on appliances and HVAC on January 1, 2026, and Connecticut and Texas added appliance coverage in July and September 2026. Independent shops get legal access to OEM parts and diagnostics they did not have before, which raises the floor on what a salvaged-part inventory is worth.

Whisper voice transcription is $0.006 per minute, with gpt-4o-mini-transcribe at $0.003 ($0.18 per hour). GPT-4o-mini vision costs roughly $0.0001 to $0.0003 per 1024 pixel image, and Gemini 2.0 Flash is cheaper still. A shop that photographs a data plate and a part, and dictates 30 seconds of condition notes on 100 parts per day, runs under 20 cents per day in inference. Two years ago that same workflow needed custom models, server costs, and a six-figure build.

At the same time, used appliance parts just became a bigger, more protected business. 13 states have enacted right-to-repair laws as of July 2026, up from 4 in 2023. About 25.75% of Americans now live under an enforceable law. California's Right to Repair Act explicitly covers appliances with a 7-year parts obligation. Colorado banned parts pairing on appliances and HVAC on January 1, 2026, and Connecticut and Texas added appliance coverage in July and September 2026. Independent shops get legal access to OEM parts and diagnostics they did not have before, which raises the floor on what a salvaged-part inventory is worth.
