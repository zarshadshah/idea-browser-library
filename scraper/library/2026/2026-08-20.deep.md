Search-and-rescue dispatch for missing pets
⏰
Perfect Timing
⚡
Unfair Advantage
+14 More

Millions of pets go missing a year, and the search is chaos. Owners post on social media and dial random numbers off Google. Thermal drone pilots, tracking dogs, and ground search teams all exist, each working alone. So the right specialist gets called on day three. By then the scent trail is cold.

Clew is a managed recovery service that runs a lost pet like a rescue mission. One call connects the owner to a trained coordinator who logs the last sighting, behavior, and scent items. From there, the coordinator builds a plan and dispatches the right specialist for the terrain: drones, scent dogs, or ground teams. Owners get time-stamped updates, real evidence like photos and coordinates, and one point of contact. Petworks lists providers and leaves the owner to coordinate them alone. Clew runs the search and owns the outcome.

Build a bench of verified local specialists in one metro: certified tracking-dog handlers, commercial drone pilots, and seasoned ground searchers. Create a simple intake form and a triage playbook that maps case type to recovery method. Launch with manual dispatch over the phone. Hardest part is quality control on the roster, since one unreliable specialist on a desperate owner's case sinks the brand. Run the first ten cases to learn what works, what breaks, and where owners get stuck. Vet clinic and shelter partnerships open the referral pipeline from day one.

Basic search packages start at $149 for a consultation and immediate next steps. Pet Protection memberships run $50 a month for priority dispatch, annual search credits, and discounted specialist rates. Vet clinics fielding lost-pet calls daily become natural referral partners. Pet insurers see fast recovery as a way to cut claim payouts. Case outcome data compounds across animal type, terrain, and time elapsed. Each recovery closed sharpens a triage algorithm that dispatches faster than the last.

*Analysis, scores, and revenue estimates are educational and based on assumptions. Results vary by execution and market conditions.
