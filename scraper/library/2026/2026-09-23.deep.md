THE IDEA

App Store submissions jumped 84% year-over-year in Q1 2026 to 235,800 new apps while downloads grew 2%. iOS CPI hit $5.84, up 19%. One solo indie dev spent a year on a game, published to both stores, and got two downloads. Another shipped a polished vocabulary app with widgets and mascots and posted publicly that he knows how to build but not how to sell. On X in September 2026, indie devs openly ask for real cross-promoters and report being scammed by fake ones. AI agents ship 10x more software; they do not ship 10x more distribution.

The idea is a curated featured-promotion network for indie mobile and desktop apps. A founder-vetted directory of live indie apps is free. A paid $199 featured campaign puts your app in front of the network for 3 days with install proof. A $49/mo membership adds SDK placement, matching rules, and monthly credits. The pitch is human curation and reported installs, not another free SDK. It is priced under Indie App Santa's $500 Growth tier to win the first yes.

App submissions jumped 84% while downloads grew 2%

The App Store took in 235,800 new app submissions in Q1 2026, an 84% year-over-year jump and the largest growth in four years, per Sensor Tower. Appfigures tracked worldwide app releases up 60% in the same quarter, with iOS alone up 80%. H1 2026 added ~560,000 new apps, nearly matching all of 2025, and the store is on track to pass one million submissions this year. The cause is not a mystery: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and Replit let one person ship what used to take a team a quarter.

Downloads did not follow. App Store downloads grew just 3% in 2025 and 2% in H1 2026. Paid acquisition got worse in the same window. Global average CPI hit $5.84 on iOS (up 19% year-over-year) and $1.92 on Android in early 2026, and total UA spend reached $78B in 2025 (up 13%), with iOS spend surging 35%. RevenueCat clocked monthly subscription app launches at 14,700+ in January 2026, up from 2,000 in January 2022. Seven times the competitors are chasing users who are barely more numerous, on channels that keep getting more expensive. Solo builders who cannot spend $5 per install need a non-paid path, and cross-promotion between non-competing apps is the one lever the AI wave itself creates: more indie apps means more inventory to swap. The counter: this same wave has already pulled in competitors. CrossPromo, ByteBrew Ads (Feb 2026), and RevenueCat's built-in cross-promo all shipped ahead of a new entrant, so the timing window is real but is closing fast, not opening.

WHY NOW

App submissions jumped 84% while downloads grew 2%

The App Store took in 235,800 new app submissions in Q1 2026, an 84% year-over-year jump and the largest growth in four years, per Sensor Tower. Appfigures tracked worldwide app releases up 60% in the same quarter, with iOS alone up 80%. H1 2026 added ~560,000 new apps, nearly matching all of 2025, and the store is on track to pass one million submissions this year. The cause is not a mystery: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and Replit let one person ship what used to take a team a quarter.

Downloads did not follow. App Store downloads grew just 3% in 2025 and 2% in H1 2026. Paid acquisition got worse in the same window. Global average CPI hit $5.84 on iOS (up 19% year-over-year) and $1.92 on Android in early 2026, and total UA spend reached $78B in 2025 (up 13%), with iOS spend surging 35%. RevenueCat clocked monthly subscription app launches at 14,700+ in January 2026, up from 2,000 in January 2022. Seven times the competitors are chasing users who are barely more numerous, on channels that keep getting more expensive. Solo builders who cannot spend $5 per install need a non-paid path, and cross-promotion between non-competing apps is the one lever the AI wave itself creates: more indie apps means more inventory to swap. The counter: this same wave has already pulled in competitors. CrossPromo, ByteBrew Ads (Feb 2026), and RevenueCat's built-in cross-promo all shipped ahead of a new entrant, so the timing window is real but is closing fast, not opening.
