Make it Pop! - the AI creative director for agencies
6.8
/10

THE IDEA

Small agencies now ship client sites with Lovable, Cursor, and Framer. Lovable alone launches 100,000 new projects a day and hit $400M ARR by February 2026. Around 40% of web designers use AI tools daily, and half of designers who use AI make full page layouts with it. The problem is on the page: purple gradient, Inter font, four cards in a grid, endless empty space. Agency operators name the exact tells on X, buyers spot the pattern, and the agency loses the retainer at the launch call.

The product is a one-page Keep / Kill / Push call sheet a strategist writes before the agency opens its AI tool. It says what the site must refuse to look like, which category defaults to use, and the one risky move worth making. Sold per project at $1,500, roughly one day of a fractional creative director. The agency still ships with the tool it already uses; the sheet steers the output instead of fixing it after.

WHY NOW

Lovable ships 100,000 new AI projects per day

AI-built client sites are the new default in agency work. Lovable hit $400M ARR by February 2026 with 25 million total projects and 100,000 new ones launching every day. Cursor reached $2B ARR in under 24 months. A survey of 15,000 developers put daily AI coding tool use at 73% of engineering teams in 2026, up from 41% in 2025. Around 40% of web designers now use AI tools every day, and half of designers who use AI use it to create complete page layouts. The workflow the customer runs is here.

The quality problem got worse in the same window. Google Labs open-sourced DESIGN.md on April 21, 2026, a file that tells Claude Code, Cursor, and Copilot how a design system should behave, because agents left alone return the statistical center: purple gradient, Inter font, four cards in a grid. Designers now write full guides on 'AI slop web design' and say a pattern that looked original in early 2025 was a cliché by mid-2026. The generation tools got cheaper and better. The judgment layer did not. That gap is the timing window: the file format for pre-generation direction exists, agencies feel the slop tax on every launch, and no one is yet selling the human calls (Keep, Kill, Push, per client, per category) as a paid product between a free template and a $15,000 sprint.
