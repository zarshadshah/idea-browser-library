WHY NOW

DAI now runs 93.6% of US podcast ad revenue

Two things changed in the last 18 months, and together they make 2026 the year to sell this service. First, the money moved. The IAB and PwC Podcast Advertising Revenue Study, released March 2026, shows dynamic ad insertion now delivers 93.6% of US podcast ad revenue. Baked-in ads have shrunk to under 10%. A live-read ad from 2023 that still runs on an old episode is not just awkward: it blocks a sellable slot in the format that now carries almost all the money. US podcast ad spend hit $2.9 billion in 2025 and is tracking to $3.0 billion in 2026, up 17.6% year over year, and the IAB raised its 2026 outlook again mid-year.

Second, the demand side opened up. Spotify launched the Spotify Ad Exchange in April 2025 and expanded automated podcast buying to 14 markets on July 11, 2025. The Trade Desk and Google DV360 can now buy podcast DAI slots programmatically. Spotify reported a 64% jump in advertisers using the exchange in the first three months. Acast, Megaphone, iHeart, and 85-plus other programmatic podcast networks are live in 2026. An empty DAI slot in a mid-tail archive can get filled by a programmatic bid in a way that was not true in 2022. On the labor side, Whisper plus LLM ad detection went commodity in 2025-2026: open-source tools (MinusPod, AGPAR, several GitHub repos) scan a 60-minute episode in under 30 minutes at a cost of about a dollar in API spend, and they output timestamped ad segments. The work that would have taken a human editor a month on a 200-episode archive is now a one-day batch job for a solo operator with an AI stack.
